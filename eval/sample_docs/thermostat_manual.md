# Nimbus N4 Smart Thermostat — User Manual

*Fictional product documentation, written for evaluating this project.*

## 1. Installation

Turn off power to your heating and cooling system at the breaker before removing the old thermostat. The N4 requires a C-wire (common wire) to supply 24 V continuous power. If your system has no C-wire, install the included Power Extender Kit (PEK-2) at the furnace control board.

Mount the base plate with the bubble level facing up, connect the labelled wires to the matching terminals (R, C, W1, Y1, G), and press the display onto the base until it clicks.

## 2. First-time setup

After power is restored the display shows the Nimbus logo for about 30 seconds. Choose your language, then select your system type: conventional (furnace and air conditioner) or heat pump. For heat pumps you must also set the O/B reversing valve to "O" (energized on cool) or "B" (energized on heat); most manufacturers other than Rheem and Ruud use "O".

## 3. Schedules

The N4 supports up to six temperature periods per day. Open Menu > Schedule, pick the days to edit, and drag each period's start time. Copy a day's schedule to other days with the "Copy to…" button. Eco Mode lowers the heating setpoint by 4 °F (2 °C) and raises the cooling setpoint by 4 °F when nobody is home, using the built-in occupancy sensor.

## 4. Filter reminders

By default the N4 reminds you to replace the air filter every 90 days of fan runtime. You can change the interval to 30, 60, 90 or 180 days under Menu > Settings > Reminders. Reset the counter after replacing the filter by pressing and holding the reminder banner for three seconds.

## 5. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E12 | Lost power on the C-wire | Check the breaker and the C-wire connection at both ends. |
| E31 | Temperature sensor fault | Restart the thermostat; if it persists, contact support for a replacement display. |
| E47 | Heat pump reversing valve did not respond | Confirm the O/B setting matches your equipment (see section 2). |
| E60 | Firmware update failed | Leave the thermostat connected to Wi-Fi; it retries automatically every 6 hours. |

## 6. Factory reset

To erase all settings, open Menu > Settings > Reset and choose "Factory reset". Alternatively, hold the display's center button for 20 seconds until the screen turns blue. A factory reset removes Wi-Fi credentials, schedules and the link to your Nimbus account.

## 7. Warranty

The Nimbus N4 carries a 3-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Professional installation by a certified Nimbus Pro installer extends the warranty to 5 years. The warranty does not cover damage from incorrect wiring or power surges.
